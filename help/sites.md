### Village buildings

- **Peasant house**: Home of peasant. %70 there is woman. 30% that house have child.
- **Weapon shop**: 3-12 weapons for sale.
- **Armor shop**: 3-12 armor for sale.
- **Barracs**: 1-3 guardians.
- **Tavern**: Bartender. Can sell bear. 1-2 adventurers for hire.

### Cost for trade goods

- Every trade good or equipment have basic cost.
- Player character buying cost basically is [250%] of basic cost and will be in range of 100 - 500.
- Player character selling cost basically is [70%] of basic cost and will be in range of 10 - 100.
- **Haggle** skill add to cost. For seller or buyer.
